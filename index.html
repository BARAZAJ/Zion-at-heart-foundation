Who we are

Family, restored one child at a time

Zion at Heart Foundation works in communities across Eastern Uganda, standing with children who have lost parents or live in vulnerable households. Rather than removing children from their communities, our team of social workers and volunteers works through guardians, schools, churches and local leaders — strengthening the families already caring for them.

That looks like walking a child through school registration, sitting with a guardian on a home visit, or making sure a household has enough food on the table. It is slow, relational work — and it is the work that lasts.

"No child should grow up alone."

Our Work

Three ways we show up

Education & School Fees

Helping children enrol and stay in school — covering term fees, scholastic materials, and the everyday cost of staying in class.

Family & Home Visits

Social workers and volunteers regularly check in with guardians, foster families and elderly caregivers — strengthening the care already happening at home.

Food & Basic Needs

Nutrition support, clothing and everyday essentials for children in households facing the greatest hardship.
